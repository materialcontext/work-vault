⚡ 5-Minute Planning Mastery

🎯 The 5-Minute Planning Process

Minute 1Read + Choose Position: Understand prompt, pick strongest argument side

Minute 2Brainstorm Reasons: List 4-5 supporting points, choose best 3

Minute 3Create Thesis: Craft clear argument with 3 preview points

Minute 4Plan Body Paragraphs: Topic sentence + example for each reason

Minute 5Counterargument + Conclusion: One opposing view + synthesis ending

✅ Position Selection

  • Choose side with most examples
  • Pick position you can argue confidently
  • Consider which has stronger evidence
  • Don't waste time on fence-sitting

💡 Quick Brainstorming

  • Write down every idea quickly
  • Don't edit while brainstorming
  • Think of specific examples and evidence
  • Choose 3 strongest, most provable points

📝 Thesis Construction

  • Use Week 1 thesis formulas
  • Include 3 preview points clearly
  • Make it arguable and specific
  • Keep it under 2 sentences

🗂️ Organization Planning

  • Arrange points from strongest to weakest
  • Match examples to each main point
  • Plan one clear counterargument
  • Outline conclusion synthesis

🎯 Planning Practice

Prompt: "Should high schools require students to take a foreign language?"

Your Challenge: Complete the 5-minute planning process. Focus on speed while maintaining quality thinking!

🏃 Efficient Drafting Without Quality Loss

📝 Introduction (3-4 minutes)

  • Hook: One engaging sentence
  • Background: 2-3 context sentences
  • Thesis: Your planned argument statement
  • Don't overthink—write and move forward

📊 Body Paragraphs (4-5 minutes each)

  • Topic sentence stating main point
  • 2-3 specific examples or evidence
  • Analysis connecting to thesis
  • Quick transition to next paragraph

⚖️ Counterargument (4-5 minutes)

  • Present opposing view fairly
  • Acknowledge its validity briefly
  • Refute with evidence
  • Strengthen your original argument

Drafting Efficiency Strategies

1Write in order: Don't jump around—complete each paragraph before moving on

2Use academic vocabulary naturally: Apply Week 4 sophisticated language automatically

3Don't stop for perfect words: Keep writing momentum, improve in revision

4Trust your planning: Follow your outline rather than second-guessing mid-draft

⚡ Speed Writing Techniques

  • Abbreviate first, expand later: "b/c" → "because" in revision
  • Use transition words immediately: Furthermore, however, consequently
  • Write specific examples quickly: Don't research perfect details
  • Maintain academic tone: Use Week 1-4 vocabulary automatically

✨ 3-Minute Speed Revision

🎯 Speed Revision Priority System

Minute 1Content Check: Thesis clear? All points support it? Counterargument included?

Minute 2Language Upgrade: Replace weak words, add sophisticated vocabulary, improve transitions

Minute 3Error Elimination: Fix obvious grammar, spelling, punctuation mistakes

🎯 Content Priorities

  • Is thesis statement clear and arguable?
  • Does each body paragraph advance thesis?
  • Is counterargument addressed?
  • Does conclusion synthesize rather than repeat?

🎭 Language Quick Fixes

  • Replace "shows" with "demonstrates"
  • Change "a lot" to "numerous" or "substantial"
  • Upgrade "things" to "factors" or "elements"
  • Add sophisticated transitions between paragraphs

🔧 Error Detection

  • Subject-verb agreement throughout
  • Consistent verb tenses
  • Proper punctuation in complex sentences
  • Spelling of key academic vocabulary

⚡ Emergency Improvements

  • Strengthen weakest body paragraph
  • Add one more sophisticated vocabulary word
  • Improve one transition between ideas
  • Fix most obvious grammatical error
Speed Revision Mantra: Perfect is the enemy of good under time pressure. Make essential improvements that have maximum impact rather than trying to fix everything!

📝 SAT Essay Excellence

SAT Essay Structure (50 minutes)

Task: Analyze how an author builds an argument in a provided passage

Focus: Evidence, reasoning, and stylistic elements that make the argument persuasive

NOT: Whether you agree with the author's position

📖 Reading + Planning (10 min)

  • Read passage carefully, noting argument structure
  • Identify 3-4 persuasive techniques used
  • Plan thesis analyzing author's rhetorical strategy
  • Choose specific textual evidence for each technique

✍️ Drafting (35 min)

  • Introduction (5 min): Summarize argument + thesis about techniques
  • Body paragraphs (25 min): One technique per paragraph with quotes
  • Conclusion (5 min): Synthesize how techniques work together

🔍 Final Review (5 min)

  • Check that analysis focuses on HOW not WHAT
  • Ensure specific evidence supports each claim
  • Fix obvious errors that hurt credibility
  • Verify sophisticated academic language

SAT Essay Success Formula

Thesis Pattern: "In [title], [author] builds a persuasive argument for [position] through [technique 1], [technique 2], and [technique 3], effectively convincing readers that [main claim]."

Body Pattern: [Author] uses [technique] to [effect]. For example, [specific quote/example]. This [technique] is effective because [analysis of impact on audience].

🎯 SAT Strategy Practice

Common rhetorical techniques to identify: emotional appeals, logical reasoning, credible evidence, expert testimony, statistical data, personal anecdotes, repetition, rhetorical questions, vivid imagery

Time Strategy: 5 min planning + 35 min writing + 5 min review

🎭 AP English Success

  • Quote selectively with line numbers
  • Connect techniques to larger themes

  • Quick proofread for major errors only

🧘 Managing Test Writing Anxiety

🎯 Before the Test

  • Practice regularly: Timed writing weekly builds confidence
  • Know the format: Understand exactly what's expected
  • Prepare materials: Multiple pens, watch, comfortable clothes
  • Get good sleep: Brain needs rest for optimal performance

⚡ During the Test

  • Read carefully: Understand prompt before starting
  • Trust your skills: Use Weeks 1-4 techniques automatically
  • Stay calm if stuck: Move to next section, return if time allows
  • Watch time but don't panic: Frequent quick time checks

🔄 When Things Go Wrong

  • Blank mind: Start with basic outline, ideas will come
  • Running out of time: Focus on completing argument, skip polish
  • Realize mistake: Quick fix if time allows, otherwise continue
  • Feeling overwhelmed: Deep breath, focus on next sentence

Confidence Building Mantras

✓"I know how to structure arguments" - Week 1 skills are automatic

✓"I can find and use evidence effectively" - Week 2 research skills transfer

✓"I can handle complex arguments" - Week 3 counterargument mastery

✓"I write with sophisticated academic voice" - Week 4 language skills

🎯 Anxiety Reduction Strategies

  • Breathing technique: 4 counts in, 4 counts hold, 4 counts out
  • Progressive muscle relaxation: Tense and release shoulder muscles
  • Positive visualization: Picture yourself writing confidently
  • Self-talk adjustment: "I'm prepared" instead of "I hope I pass"
