🏹 Members of the Powhatan Confederacy

🏛️ A Complex Political System

Members of the Powhatan Confederacy strongly resented the presence of the English. They viewed them as invaders who posed a threat to the continuation of their own indigenous culture and to their land.

👑 Political Structure

  • Over 14,000 people under Chief Powhatan
  • 30+ tribes in the confederacy
  • Sophisticated leadership hierarchy
  • Tribute system connecting tribes

🌾 Economic System

  • Agriculture: corn, beans, squash
  • Seasonal hunting and fishing
  • Trade networks with other tribes
  • Sustainable resource management

⚔️ English Relations

  • Initial curiosity turned to resistance
  • Conflicts over land and resources
  • Pocahontas as cultural bridge
  • Eventually overwhelmed by numbers

📚 Key Vocabulary - Chapter 3

Proprietary Colony
A colony owned by an individual who received it from the king. Maryland was the first proprietary colony, owned by Lord Baltimore.
Powhatan Confederacy
A political alliance of about 30 Algonquian-speaking tribes in Virginia, led by Chief Powhatan, with over 14,000 people.
Indentured Servants
People who worked for a set period (usually 4-7 years) to pay off their passage to America. After their service, they were free.
Cash Crops
Crops grown specifically to sell for profit, not for personal use. Tobacco, rice, and indigo were major colonial cash crops.
Mayflower Compact
The first written agreement for self-government in America, signed by the Pilgrims in 1620 before landing at Plymouth.
Puritans
English Protestants who wanted to "purify" the Church of England. They founded Massachusetts Bay Colony in 1630.
Great Migration
The movement of thousands of English Puritans to New England in the 1630s to escape religious persecution.
Toleration Act
Maryland's 1649 law that protected religious freedom for all Christians, one of the first such laws in colonial America.
Quakers
Members of the Religious Society of Friends who believed in equality, pacifism, and direct relationship with God. They founded Pennsylvania.
Middle Passage
The forced voyage of enslaved Africans across the Atlantic Ocean to the Americas, part of the triangular trade system.
House of Burgesses
Virginia's representative assembly, established in 1619. It was the first elected legislative body in colonial America.
Plantation System
Large agricultural estates that relied on enslaved labor to grow cash crops, especially in the Southern colonies.
