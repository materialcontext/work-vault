🔧 Why These Work

These frames work for physics, chemistry, biology, environmental science, and more. Learn once, use everywhere!

🔄 Cause & Effect Frames

Frame 1: "This happens because..."
"This happens because reason."
Exothermic reactions get hot because more energy is released than absorbed.
Ice melts because thermal energy breaks hydrogen bonds.
Plants need sunlight because photosynthesis requires energy input.
Frame 2: "When X occurs, Y results..."
"When cause occurs, effect results."
When bonds form, energy is released.
When temperature rises, reaction rates increase.
When pressure increases, gas volume decreases.

👁️ Evidence & Observation Frames

Frame 3: "We can observe..."
"We can observe evidence when situation."
We can observe temperature increase when combustion occurs.
We can observe gas bubbles when metals react with acid.
We can observe color changes when indicators meet acids.
Frame 4: "This shows that..."
"Evidence shows that conclusion."
Rising temperature shows that energy is being released.
Bubble formation shows that gas is being produced.
pH changes show that acids and bases are reacting.

📚 Example & Comparison Frames

Frame 5: "For example..."
"For example, specific case demonstrates concept."
For example, burning wood demonstrates exothermic reactions.
For example, melting ice demonstrates endothermic processes.
For example, rusting iron demonstrates slow oxidation.
Frame 6: "This is different from..."
"X is different from Y because key difference."
Exothermic reactions are different from endothermic ones because they release energy.
Chemical changes are different from physical changes because new substances form.
Strong acids are different from weak acids because they ionize completely.

💡 Explanation & Clarification Frames

Frame 7: "The reason is..."
"The reason observation happens is underlying cause."
The reason hand warmers get hot is iron oxidation releases energy.
The reason cold packs get cold is the process absorbs thermal energy.
The reason catalysts work is they lower activation energy.
Frame 8: "In other words..."
"In other words, simpler explanation."
In other words, exothermic reactions give off heat energy.
In other words, endothermic reactions take in heat energy.
In other words, catalysts make reactions happen faster.
