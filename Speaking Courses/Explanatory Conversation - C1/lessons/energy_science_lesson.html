🔗 Chemical Bonds and Energy

The Fundamental Energy Rules

All chemical reactions follow two universal energy principles

⚡ Rule 1: Breaking Bonds

REQUIRES ENERGY INPUT
  • Bonds are attractive forces between atoms
  • Energy must overcome these attractions
  • Think: pulling apart magnets requires work
  • Always an endothermic process
A—B + Energy → A + B

⚡ Rule 2: Forming Bonds

RELEASES ENERGY
  • Atoms form bonds to become more stable
  • Energy is released as bonds form
  • Think: magnets snapping together
  • Always an exothermic process
A + B → A—B + Energy

🔥 Exothermic Reactions: More Energy Out

When Bond Formation Wins

Energy released from new bonds > Energy needed to break old bonds

🧪 Example: Combustion of Methane

CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O + ENERGY
Energy Analysis:
Bonds Broken: 4 C-H + 2 O=O = 2648 kJ
Bonds Formed: 2 C=O + 4 O-H = 3470 kJ
Net Energy Released: 822 kJ/mol
  • Strong C=O and O-H bonds form
  • Much more energy released than used
  • Excess energy appears as heat and light

📊 Energy Profile

Energy ↑
    |
    |  Reactants
    |      ↘
    |          Products
    |_________________→
                 Reaction Progress

Products have LOWER energy than reactants

❄️ Endothermic Reactions: More Energy In

When Bond Breaking Wins

Energy needed to break old bonds > Energy released from new bonds

🧪 Example: Thermal Decomposition

CaCO₃ + ENERGY → CaO + CO₂
Energy Analysis:
Bonds Broken: Ca-O + C=O = 1420 kJ
Bonds Formed: Ca-O (new) = 1240 kJ
Net Energy Required: 180 kJ/mol
  • Strong ionic bonds must be broken
  • New bonds are weaker than original
  • Energy must be continuously supplied

📊 Energy Profile

Energy ↑
    |            Products
    |         ↗
    |  Reactants
    |
    |_________________→
                 Reaction Progress

Products have HIGHER energy than reactants

🏔️ Activation Energy: The Energy Barrier

Why Reactions Need a "Push" to Start

Even exothermic reactions need initial energy input

🔥 Why Paper Doesn't Spontaneously Burn

  • Combustion is highly exothermic
  • But it needs activation energy to start
  • Match provides the initial energy
  • Once started, reaction sustains itself

📊 Complete Energy Profile

Energy ↑
    |       ⛰️ Activation
    |  Reactants   Energy
    |            ↘
    |              Products
    |_________________→
                 Reaction Progress

🧪 Molecular Explanation

  • Molecules must collide with enough energy
  • Bonds partially break in transition state
  • Requires precise molecular orientations
  • Temperature increases molecular motion
Collision Theory:
Effective Collision = Right Energy + Right Orientation

🌍 Energy Changes in Your World

🔥 Exothermic Examples

  • Cellular Respiration: Glucose + O₂ → Energy for life
  • Hand Warmers: Iron oxidation releases heat
  • Car Engines: Gasoline combustion powers motion
  • Batteries: Chemical energy → electrical energy

❄️ Endothermic Examples

  • Photosynthesis: Light energy stores glucose
  • Cold Packs: Ammonium nitrate dissolution
  • Cooking: Heat breaks down proteins
  • Thermal Decomposition: Limestone → Lime

🏠 Everyday Examples

  • Exo: Baking soda + vinegar
  • Endo: Evaporating sweat cools you
  • Exo: Concrete setting releases heat
  • Endo: Ice melting absorbs energy

⚖️ Energy Conservation in Reactions

Energy Cannot Be Created or Destroyed

It only changes form during chemical reactions

🔄 Energy Transformations

  • Chemical → Heat: Combustion reactions
  • Chemical → Light: Chemiluminescence
  • Chemical → Electrical: Batteries
  • Light → Chemical: Photosynthesis

📊 Energy Accounting

For ANY reaction:
Energy In = Energy Out

Exothermic:
Chemical Energy → Heat + Light + Products

Endothermic:
Heat + Reactants → Products + Stored Energy

⚡ Catalysts: Energy Shortcuts

Making Reactions Faster Without Changing Energy

Catalysts lower activation energy but don't change overall energy change

🧪 How Catalysts Work

  • Provide alternative reaction pathway
  • Lower activation energy barrier
  • Speed up forward AND reverse reactions
  • Regenerated unchanged after reaction

📊 Catalyst Energy Profile

Energy ↑
    |     ⛰️ Without catalyst

    |            ↘

    |_________________→
Same overall energy change!

🧬 Biological Catalysts

Enzymes speed up reactions in living things by factors of millions!

🏭 Industrial Catalysts

Platinum in car catalytic converters breaks down pollutants

🌡️ Temperature Effects on Energy

Why Hot Things React Faster

Temperature affects molecular motion and collision frequency

🔥 Higher Temperature Effects

  • Molecules move faster
  • More frequent collisions
  • More collisions exceed activation energy
  • Reaction rate increases exponentially
Rule of Thumb:
+10°C → Double reaction rate

❄️ Lower Temperature Effects

  • Molecules move slower
  • Fewer effective collisions
  • Preserving food by cooling
  • Chemical storage requires cool conditions
Why refrigeration works:
Slows decomposition reactions
